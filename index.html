Astraea Station Version History
v2.0 — Executive Economy & Crisis Expansion (Current)
  • Polis Credits Economy: Earn 25+ credits per cycle to spend on upgrades and decrees.
  • Random Crisis Events: Solar Flares, Atmospheric Leaks, and Agora Riots randomly occur.
  • District Upgrades: Spend credits to level up Solarium (+Efficiency), Lyceum (+Debate output), and Hydroponics (+Oxygen generation).

  • In-Game Changelog: Added interactive HUD modal to view project version history.

  • Positioned AI Advisor chatbot outside the habitat simulation viewport.

  • Created 3-column executive UI layout for field commands and diagnostic prompts.
